01 Mandatory Road Traffic Safety Rules (Motor Vehicles Act & Highway Code)
RULE 101 • ABSOLUTE CRITICAL

Stopline Compliance & Amber Signal Dilemma Zone

Vehicles must come to a complete halt before the painted stopline when the amber or red phase activates. The Dilemma Zone is algorithmically mitigated by the SignalSync 3.5s All-Red clearance interval to prevent broadside (T-bone) collisions.

Penalty: Section 184 (Dangerous Driving) Zero Tolerance
RULE 102 • STATUTORY RIGHT-OF-WAY

Emergency Vehicle Preemption Protocol (Corridor 108/112)

All motor traffic in adjacent and forward lanes must safely yield right-of-way by moving to the extreme left curb immediately upon hearing emergency siren frequencies (750 Hz - 1.2 kHz) or when SignalSync broadcasts an automated Green Wave priority corridor.

Section 194E MVA: ₹10,000 fine / License Suspension Automatic Green Wave
RULE 103 • VULNERABLE ROAD USERS

Pedestrian Crosswalk Priority (Pelican / Zebra Zones)

Pedestrians possess unconditional right-of-way on designated Zebra crossings. Traffic turning left or right on green must decelerate to under 15 km/h and yield to crossing pedestrians. Blind corner sensors actively trigger flashing beacons.

IRC: 67-2012 Pedestrian Standards VRU Protected
RULE 104 • SPEED HARMONIZATION

Variable Speed Limits & Stopping Sight Distance (SSD)

Maintain the 2-Second rule under normal pavement friction and the 4-Second rule during wet precipitation. On urban expressways (WEH, Outer Ring Road), speed traps dynamically synchronize with traffic density to eliminate stop-and-go shockwaves.
